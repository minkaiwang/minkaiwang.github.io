Minkai Wang (王珉锴) is a Ph.D. student in Educational Technology at Zhejiang University. His research spans game-based learning, artificial intelligence in education, and esports education. During his master's studies, he undertook joint training in esports education at the Future Imaging Laboratory, Zhejiang University Yangtze River Delta Smart Oasis Innovation Center. He currently serves as an R&D Engineer at the Zhejiang University Yangtze River Delta Smart Oasis–NetDragon International Joint R&D Center for Esports Education and as an External Expert at the Zhejiang-Netherlands Joint Laboratory on Digital Diagnosis and Treatment of Oral Diseases, Hangzhou.

His research examines how large language models, virtual agents, adaptive mechanisms, and digital games can reshape learning experience, engagement, cognitive load, and behavioral patterns. His academic work has appeared in Journal of Computer Assisted Learning, Journal of Educational Computing Research, and Entertainment Computing.
