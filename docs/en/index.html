    Minkai Wang

    Minkai Wang (王珉锴) works at the intersection of game-based learning, artificial intelligence in education, metaverse learning environments, and esports education. He will begin his Ph.D. study in Educational Technology at Zhejiang University in September 2026, supervised by Prof. Xuesong Zhai, and is jointly trained at the Future Imaging Laboratory and the ZJU International Joint R&D Center for Esports Education.

    His research examines how large language models, virtual agents, adaptive mechanisms, and digital games can reshape learning experience, engagement, cognitive load, and behavioral patterns. His academic work has appeared in Journal of Computer Assisted Learning, Journal of Educational Computing Research, and Entertainment Computing.
