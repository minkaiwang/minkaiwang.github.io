    Minkai Wang

    Minkai Wang (王珉锴) is a Ph.D. student in Educational Technology at Zhejiang University (enrolled September 2026), supervised by Prof. Xuesong Zhai. His research spans game-based learning, artificial intelligence in education, metaverse learning environments, and esports education. He was jointly trained at the Future Imaging Laboratory from March to July 2026 and continues his joint training at the ZJU International Joint R&D Center for Esports Education.

    His research examines how large language models, virtual agents, adaptive mechanisms, and digital games can reshape learning experience, engagement, cognitive load, and behavioral patterns. His academic work has appeared in Journal of Computer Assisted Learning, Journal of Educational Computing Research, and Entertainment Computing.
